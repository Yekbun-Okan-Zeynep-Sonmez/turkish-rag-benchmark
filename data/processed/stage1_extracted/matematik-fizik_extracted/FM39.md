## A NUMERICAL ANALYSIS OF NATURAL CONVECTION IN A L-SHAPED ENCLOSURE

## ABSTRACT

In  this  study,  natural  convection  in  a  L-shaped  enclosure  was  investigated  numerically.  The  left  hand  side of enclosure is cooler than the right hand side of wall. Bottom and top walls are insulated. Governing equaitons for natural convection have been integrated over a finite control volume to obtain finite difference equations. For the solution a computer program was developed using SIMPLEM algorithm was used. Calculations were performed in the range of 10 4 &lt;Ra&lt;10 6 and for different L-shaped dimesions.

Key Words : Natural convection, SIMPLEM algorithm, L shaped enclosure
