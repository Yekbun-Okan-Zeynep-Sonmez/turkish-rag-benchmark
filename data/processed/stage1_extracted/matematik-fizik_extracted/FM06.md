## Step Size Strategy Based On Error Analysis

Abstract: In  this  study;  a  step  size  strategy  based  on  error  analysis  is  obtained  for Runge-  Kutta  method  of  order  2.  Depending  on  this  strategy  two  algorithms  which determine constant or variable step size according to structure of problem and calculate numerical solutions in each step are given.

Key words: Step size, Numerical integration, Step size strategy, Step size control
