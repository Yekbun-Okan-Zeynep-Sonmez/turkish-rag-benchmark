## THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EMOTIONAL INTELLIGENCE AND FAMILY FUNCTIONS

## ABSTRACT

The  main  purpose  of  this  research  is  to investigate  the  relationship between  family  functions  and  emotional intelligence. Subjects  were  274 parents  (152  female,  122  male). EQ/hyphenminusNED was  applied  to  sample  group  to determine their emotional intelligence and Family Assessment Device (FAD/ADÖ)  was  applied  to  determine  family  functions.  The  relationship between  emotional  intelligence  and  family  functions  was investigated  by using Pearson Moment Correlation Coefficent Techniques.
