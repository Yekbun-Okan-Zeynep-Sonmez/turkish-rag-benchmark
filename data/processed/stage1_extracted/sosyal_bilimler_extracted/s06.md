## Criticisms against the Theories of Modernization and Modernism

This  paper  attempts to discuss the  phenomenon of modernization from the perspective  of  the  change  occurring  in  such  fields  as  society,  politics,  culture,  and religion.  It  also  tries  to  provide  a  critical  insight  into  the  modernization  theories, offering an exposition of the historical affairs, furnishing a comparative analysis of the contemporary theories of religion as well as of the relationships between religion and society, and presenting a social, economic and cultural background of the modernization phenomenon. Modernization is a multifaceted process that encompasses many elements such as industrialization in business life, mass migrations from rural areas to big cities, the evolution of communities into societies, the rise of individualism and egalitarianism, the rationalization of minds and the organization of society. Modernization has given rise to a new way of life and a new manner of social organization, of which the effect has been observed worldwide. Since the nineteenth century,  many  sociologists  have  tended  to  view  social  change  as  a  uniform  and inescapable  process.  Many  modern  intellectuals,  however,  have  challenged  the common conviction that modernization is inevitably historical and uniform.

Key Words: Modernization, rationalization, individualism, religion, the change theories
