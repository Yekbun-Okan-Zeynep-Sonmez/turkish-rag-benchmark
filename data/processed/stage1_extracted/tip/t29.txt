ABS TRACT With the understanding of tumor immunology, many specific new treatment steps in the 
treatment of cancer come to the fore. Defects in the immune system cause cancer cells to not be de-
stroyed, to multiply uncontrollably, to spread, and to death. As a result of research carried out based on 
this, immunotherapy has become one of the first drugs in cancer treatment today. The most important 
examples of immunotherapy used in practice are; monoclonal antibodies, immune checkpoint inhibitors, 
some cytokines, oncolytic virus therapy, adoptive immunotherapy and cancer vaccines. In this article, 
information on cancer immunotherapy has been compiled in the light of current literature.
