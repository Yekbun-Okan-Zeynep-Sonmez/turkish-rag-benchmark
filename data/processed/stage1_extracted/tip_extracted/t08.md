## REVIEW: PHYSICAL ACTIVITY AND CORONARY HEART DISEASE RISK FACTORS

The  present  review  discusses  in  detail  the  positive  effects  of physical activity observed in the prevention and treatment of cardiovascular  system  diseases,  which  constitute  the  leading  cause  of death  in  most  countries.
